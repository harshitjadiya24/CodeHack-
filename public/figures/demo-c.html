How lending works

Seven steps, each written to the record as it happens. Nobody can quietly change what was agreed.

i

Find an object

Search by department, room, or what you’re making.

ii

Check your clearance

Your training and year decide what you can borrow, and you’re told why.

iii

Request a slot

Choose a time and say what it’s for.

iv

The draw, if it’s full

A sealed, weighted draw that anyone can re-run.

v

The keeper approves

Every decision comes with a written reason.

vi

Hand over

Three photographs on collection; condition agreed by both sides.

vii

Return

Photographs again. Damage goes to a fair review.

When six people want the same hour

Asking first doesn’t win. Everyone in time is entered; those who’ve had less this month count a little more.
