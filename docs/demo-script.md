# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Use the prod build (`NEXT_DIST_DIR=.next-prod npx next start -p 3100`) with `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_MODE=1`, so check-in works at any time. Every account uses the password `Saajha@2026`; on `/login` you can just click the role cards.

## 0:00 The problem (15 s)

Start on the landing page and let the live shelf scroll.

> "Indian colleges own lakhs of rupees of equipment that sits idle. Oscilloscopes, 3D printers and seminar halls are booked on WhatsApp and paper registers. Nobody knows what's free, the same people get everything, and nothing is recorded. Baanto fixes that."

## 0:15 A student finds and books (40 s)

Sign in as **Meera** (student).

1. Discover opens on the closest open slots. Switch the scope to *My city* to show partner campuses on the map.
2. Open the oscilloscope. The eligibility panel shows each check passing, with its reason.
3. Pick a slot and send the request. Open the booking and show the decision receipt and the **Add to calendar** button.

> "Every rule explains itself. Meera sees why she can book, not just that she can."

## 0:55 Fair when it's popular (30 s)

Sign in as **Priya** (lab in-charge).

1. The request queue shows each request's priority and *why*: a deadline, first-time use, recent strikes.
2. Open a fair draw. Show the commit hash, run it, then open the public verify page.

> "When two people want the same slot, a seeded draw decides, and anyone can re-run it to check it was fair."

## 1:25 Trust that travels (25 s)

Sign in as **Aarav**. Open a training-gated resource: Baanto names the exact badge he is missing and how to get it.

Then show **Zoya** from Riverside asking for an endorsement for a partner campus instead of being refused. Open the Trust Passport and its QR.

## 1:50 The admin office (45 s)

Sign in as **IQAC** (admin office).

1. `/admin`: the utilisation heatmap, then **Mostly idle this month**. Tap a nudge such as *Turn on instant approval* and say that the owner is told.
2. **Next week** forecast.
3. **Import from spreadsheets**: upload the inventory template. Show the row-by-row check and the *List N resources* button. Don't confirm on camera.
4. **NAAC report**: pick the academic year, then show print and the CSV download.

> "Setting up a college takes an afternoon: two spreadsheets. And the NAAC Criterion IV report writes itself from real records."

## 2:35 Built to be trusted (15 s)

Open `/audit/verify`: the hash chain is intact. Switch the header to **हिन्दी** and back.

> "Every action is on an append-only audit chain, row-level security protects the data, and it works in Hindi and offline on a phone."

## 2:50 Close (10 s)

Back to the landing page.

> "Baanto. Share what your campus already owns."

## Before recording

- Use a clean browser profile at 1440×900, in light theme.
- Don't confirm imports, verify the register, or plan maintenance on camera. Those change the live demo data.
